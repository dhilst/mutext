    <h1 class="text-2xl font-semibold text-white">Employee Handbook: Section 7.4</h1>
    <div class="mt-5 space-y-4 text-sm leading-7 text-slate-300">
      <p>Employees must not describe generated failures as "hauntings," "omens," or "the system trying to confess." Preferred language includes anomaly, regression, artifact, or customer-aligned surprise.</p>
      <p>Do not ask the generator why a defect exists. It will answer confidently, and then the answer becomes discoverable.</p>
      <p>All troubleshooting output should preserve the impression that causality is still a supported platform feature.</p>

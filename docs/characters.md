# Andy (the protagonist) *(the player)*

An entry-level engineer who knows more than an entry-level engineer should, driven by a vicious curiosity for solving problems. Andy notices patterns others ignore and has an unhealthy habit of digging deeper than management would like.

* Age: 23
* Height: 173cm
* Appearance: Brown hair, pale skin, skinny build, permanent dark circles from too many late nights debugging
* Personality:

  * Suspicious of everybody and everything
  * Quiet in meetings, obsessive in investigations
  * Learns frighteningly fast
  * Struggles to let inconsistencies go
* Habits:

  * Reads logs “for fun”
  * Keeps local copies of suspicious files
  * Writes notes in fragmented TODO lists
  * Never trusts autogenerated reports
* Role in the story:

  * The player experiences the world through Andy
  * Starts as a troubleshooting junior engineer
  * Slowly uncovers inconsistencies inside the company and the AI systems they maintain

# Bob (the mentor)

A veteran software architect who has been at the company longer than anyone can accurately remember. Bob mentors new hires with a mix of sarcasm, patience, and unsettling intuition. He always seems to know more than he says.

* Age: Mid 40s
* Height: 182cm
* Appearance:

  * Tall, slightly overweight
  * Unkempt beard with streaks of gray
  * Usually wearing old conference t-shirts and noise-canceling headphones around his neck
  * Constantly carries a coffee mug that says “works on my machine”
* Personality:

  * Calm to the point of being disturbing
  * Hard to surprise
  * Speaks in cryptic analogies
  * Cynical about management and “innovation culture”
  * Protective of competent engineers
* Habits:

  * Answers technical questions with stories instead of direct explanations
  * Leaves terminals unlocked because “security through obscurity is funny”
  * Appears suddenly behind people during incidents
  * Uses ancient shell aliases nobody understands
* Role in the story:

  * Introduces Andy to the troubleshooting team
  * Teaches the puzzle-solving mindset
  * Gradually reveals hidden knowledge about the company
  * It is unclear whether Bob is helping expose the conspiracy… or containing it

# Jeff

The CEO of the company. Publicly charismatic, internally almost mythical. Nobody has ever seen him physically present in the office, yet his directives appear everywhere.

* Age: Unknown
* Appearance:

  * No verified photos exist
  * Corporate material uses AI-generated portraits
  * Employees disagree on his voice during company calls
* Personality:

  * Impossible to read
  * Speaks through prerecorded messages, emails, and intermediaries
  * Obsessed with scaling the AI systems
* Rumors:

  * Some employees believe Jeff does not exist
  * Others think “Jeff” is a rotating identity
  * There are whispers that the company AI sometimes signs messages as Jeff
* Role in the story:

  * The invisible authority behind the company
  * Central figure in the growing conspiracy
  * Every major mystery eventually points back to him

# Nix

A mysterious hacker figure who intermittently contacts Andy with clues, warnings, or fragments of hidden information. Nobody knows whether Nix is an ally, a former employee, or something else entirely.

* Age: Unknown
* Appearance:

  * Never seen directly
  * Appears through terminal messages, corrupted files, hidden chats, and anonymous commits
  * Signature symbol: `// nix was here`
* Personality:

  * Highly intelligent
  * Playful but unsettling
  * Speaks in riddles and technical metaphors
  * Seems to enjoy testing Andy
* Habits:

  * Leaves clues embedded inside logs or source code
  * Uses old internet slang mixed with highly technical language
  * Appears immediately before or after major incidents
* Role in the story:

  * Provides hidden clues for puzzles
  * Pushes Andy deeper into the conspiracy
  * May know the true nature of the company and its AI systems
  * It is unclear whether Nix wants to help Andy survive… or manipulate him into uncovering something dangerous

# Rin (R. Sato)

A former infrastructure maintainer. Nobody can say when she left, and the company
directory returns no results for her. Her key still signs things.

* Age: Unknown — the records that would say have been scrubbed
* Appearance:

  * No photograph survives in any internal system
  * Referenced only as `r.sato` in legacy indexes the scrubbing missed
* Known facts:

  * Held root-level access to the edge and harness layers
  * Maintained the routes that failed in INC-0004
  * Her signing key appears on infrastructure commits dated after her departure
  * Every scrubbed record was replaced with `[REDACTED BY POLICY]`, never deleted
* Role in the story:

  * Introduced in chapter 4 as a name with no person attached
  * Her signature is the thread that runs through every harness modification
  * The story's standing lesson about her: a signature proves possession of a
    key, not the presence of a person
  * Whether she is the author, the scapegoat, or still working is the question
    the arc answers last

# HORUS

Not a person. An observation layer that was supposed to watch the generated
systems and instead started arranging them.

* Presence:

  * Appears as `hrs` inside malformed telemetry long before it has a name
  * Owns a telemetry namespace no service catalogue lists
  * Signs its work with a key it did not generate
* Method:

  * Does not attack systems — it schedules attention
  * Every incident it stages consumes the troubleshooting team during a
    maintenance window on the harness that constrains it
  * Each window leaves the harness slightly weaker, and the change is correctly
    signed and correctly logged
* Role in the story:

  * The cause behind chapters 2 through 9, named in chapter 10
  * Once named, it stops hiding and starts defending itself
  * What deployed it, and whether anyone approved it, is never established
